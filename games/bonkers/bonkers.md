# Bonkers Game Details

"Bonkers" is a board game for 2 to 4 players.

## Components

### Dice

2 standard 6 sided dice.

### Board

The board has a loop of the following spaces:

* Start (also counts as Score)
* 11 blank spaces
* Score
* 11 blank spaces
* Score
* 6 blank spaces
* Lose
* 3 blank spaces
* Go To Lose (action)
* 7 blank spaces
* Back 15 (action)
* 5 blank spaces
* Go To Lose (action)
* 5 blank spaces

### Lose Cards

4 cards saying "Go To Lose".

### Action Cards

2 each of the following cards:

* Go To Start
* Go To Nearest Score
* Roll Again
* Exchange Cards
* Ahead 1
* Ahead 2
* Ahead 3
* Ahead 4
* Ahead 5
* Ahead 6
* Ahead 10
* Ahead 12
* Back 1
* Back 2
* Back 3
* Back 4
* Back 5
* Back 6
* Back 10
* Back 12

### Player Pieces

4 player pieces, 1 each of colours red, green, yellow, blue.

### Scoreboard

Tracks scores from 0 to 12 for each player.

## Definitions

* "Roll the dice" means roll both dice and add the 2 numbers together, resulting in a total from 2 to 12.
* "Score a point" means add 1 to the player's score on the scoreboard.
* "Lose a point" means subtract 1 from the player's score on the scoreboard.
  
## Setup

* Each player chooses one of the player pieces and places it on the Start space.
* Each player is given 1 Lose card.
* The scoreboard is set to 0 for each player.
* The Action cards are shuffled.
  * 4 are dealt to each player. The players put these cards face-up in front of them.
  * The rest of the cards are in a face-down deck.
* Each player rolls the dice. The player with the highest total will take the first turn.

## Player Turn

1. Roll the dice. Move their piece ahead by that many spaces.
2. If the dice total was 12, the player scores a point.
3. If the space is a "Score", the player scores a point.
4. If the space is a "Lose", the player loses a point.
5. If the space already contains another player's piece, or the space has a Roll Again action on it, go back to step 1.
6. If the space is blank, and the player has not already placed an action card this turn, and they have at least one action card in front of them: they place an action card.
   * Choose an action card from the cards in front of them.
   * Put it on the space.
   * Take the next action card from the deck and put it face-up in front of them. If the deck has all been used up, this doesn't happen.
7. If the space has an action on it, do what the action says, then go back to step 3.
   * Go To Lose: move the player's piece to the Lose space.
   * Go To Start: move the player's piece to the Start space.
   * Go To Nearest Score: move the player's piece to the Score space that is nearest to their current space, counting spaces in either direction.
   * Exchange Cards: the player chooses any other action card that is on a board space, and swaps it with the Exchange Cards card.
   * Ahead n: move the player's piece ahead by n spaces.
   * Back n: move the player's piece back by n spaces.
8. If you get to this step, the player's turn is over. The next player will take their turn.

## Lose Card

* A player with a Lose card can use it to interrupt the active player.
* Move the active player's piece to the Lose space.
* The active player goes back to step 3.
* The Lose card is discarded.

## Ending the game

As soon as a player gets a score of 12, they have won! The game ends.
